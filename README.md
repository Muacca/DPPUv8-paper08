# Reading the sign of a Nieh–Yan charge on compact spin three-manifolds

Muacca · DPPUv8 Paper 08 · Manuscript v0.1 · 22 November 2026

A spatial Dirac probe on a charged homogeneous slice experiences a constant axial shift. On a reference Sol torus bundle the two first nonzero-fibre thresholds agree exactly when the least positive and least absolute negative values of an integral binary form agree. This criterion holds for arbitrary positive hyperbolic trace. The coincidence of chirality and threshold splitting is verified for 488 primitive monodromy conjugacy classes up to trace 60. One massive Dirac shell on an expanding Nil quotient gives a separate dynamical example: at fixed common endpoint projectors, the two signed charges give occupations of about 0.168451 and 0.042475 per copy.

The article gives the proof of the criterion, explicit counterexamples relating chirality and spectral asymmetry, the lift-dependent round-sphere table, and the definition and error bounds of the finite-shell experiment. The signed quantities use $p_\theta=-Q_\Sigma$. The Nil data column labelled plus denotes positive velocity constant and therefore negative NY charge.
