# Scope of the results

The Sol theorem fixes the unit-area fibre reference metric, the invariant-frame spin lift, positive hyperbolic monodromy and nonzero fibre momentum. The trace bound applies to the census of 488 primitive classes, its 472 chiral entries and six chiral entries with eta zero. Complex kernel dimensions count oriented lattice orbits.

Nil’s principal thresholds exclude centre-zero momentum; full-space thresholds are stored separately. For spherical data the canonical order-seven lens row has eta +4/7; the mirrored rotation-weight convention used in lens.csv has eta −4/7. All eta values omit zero modes. The Seifert data are fixed-metric controls. They do not classify general hyperbolic-base Seifert spectra.

The occupation calculation takes the same initial and final LC projectors for both signed charges. It measures the particle side, per copy, with the given mass and finite interval. The probability enclosure does not establish a whole-Fock-space result. The source checks and the calculations here are internal computational verification.
